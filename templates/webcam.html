Hints

  • Hold the gesture for about 1 second until it is counted.
  • Lower your arms before the next gesture – a held gesture counts only once.
  • Raise only ONE hand; keep the other below your face. Both hands up do not count.
  • Face the camera, head and shoulders visible. Left/right is your own left/right.
  • For Undo: arms straight out to the sides at shoulder height
  • After a point, further point gestures are ignored for the set pause. Undo always works.
  • The closer and larger you are in the picture, the more reliable. Check below the picture whether a person is detected.
  • With a detection area (ROI), only people standing inside it are counted.
